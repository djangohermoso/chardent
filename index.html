Services

Everything your smile needs, under one roof

From routine check-ups to full smile makeovers — planned around you, never rushed.

Cleaning

Oral prophylaxis to lift plaque, tartar, and stains — the foundation of a healthy mouth.

Fillings (Pasta)

Tooth-coloured fillings that stop decay and rebuild the natural shape of the tooth.

Braces

Metal and ceramic braces with a clear plan to straighten your bite and your smile.

Cosmetics & Veneers

Veneers, bonding, and reshaping to refine the colour and form of your front teeth.

Root canal (RCT)

Root canal treatment that saves a badly infected tooth and puts an end to the pain.

Dentures

Custom full and partial dentures fitted for a comfortable, natural-looking result.

Oral surgery

Simple and surgical extractions, including wisdom teeth, done carefully and gently.

Gum treatment

Care for bleeding, swollen, or receding gums to protect the bone underneath.

Dental implants

Titanium implants that replace missing teeth with a permanent, stable foundation.

Digital x-rays

Low-radiation digital imaging for an accurate diagnosis in just a few seconds.
